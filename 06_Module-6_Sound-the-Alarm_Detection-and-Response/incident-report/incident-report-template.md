## Findings
For each finding: ID, observation, evidence, alternative explanations, confidence, and impact. Distinguish suspected compromise from confirmed compromise.

## Response
Separate proposed actions from actions performed. Record owner, time, result, and rollback.

## Recovery and verification
Record success criteria, actual test results, and monitoring needs.

## Limitations and lessons
Document missing logs, timestamp assumptions, untested hypotheses, and follow-up work.
